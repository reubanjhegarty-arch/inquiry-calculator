Car Financing Tips

Shorter terms save money: While a 72 or 84-month loan lowers your monthly repayment, you pay significantly more in total interest.

The 20/4/10 Rule: Put down at least 20%, finance for no more than 4 years, and ensure total vehicle costs (loan, fuel, insurance) stay under 10% of your gross monthly income.
