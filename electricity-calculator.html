How Electricity is Calculated

Formula: Energy (kWh) = (Power in Watts × Hours used) ÷ 1,000.

Total Cost: Energy (kWh) × Cost per kWh.

Example: A 2,000W heater run for 5 hours a day consumes 10 kWh daily. At $0.30 per kWh, that costs $3.00/day or $90.00/month.
