About Sales Tax

Add Tax: Multiply the pre-tax price by (1 + tax rate) to get the final cost.

Reverse Tax: Divide the total by (1 + tax rate) to find the original pre-tax price.

Find Rate: Divide the tax amount by the pre-tax price and multiply by 100.

US Sales Tax: Rates vary by state, county, and city. Common combined rates range from 0% (Oregon, Montana) to over 10% (parts of Louisiana, Tennessee).

Frequently Asked Questions

What states have no sales tax?
Five US states have no statewide sales tax: Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, and Oregon. However, Alaska allows local jurisdictions to charge sales tax.
Is sales tax the same as VAT?
No. Sales tax is added at the point of sale to the consumer. VAT (Value Added Tax) is collected at every stage of production. The US uses sales tax; most other countries use VAT.
What is the average US sales tax rate?
The average combined state and local sales tax rate in the US is approximately 6.6%, though individual rates range from 0% to over 10%.
