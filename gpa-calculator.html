How GPA is Calculated

GPA Formula: (Sum of (Grade Points × Credits)) ÷ Total Credits

Grade Scale (4.0): A=4.0, A-=3.7, B+=3.3, B=3.0, B-=2.7, C+=2.3, C=2.0, D=1.0, F=0.0

Frequently Asked Questions

What's a good GPA?
A GPA of 3.5 or higher is typically considered excellent. Most colleges want a 3.0+ GPA.
Does this calculator include weighted GPA?
Yes, you can assign credit hours to each course to calculate weighted GPA.
