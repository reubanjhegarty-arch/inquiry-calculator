What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) estimates body fat using your weight relative to your height. It's a quick screening tool used by the WHO and doctors worldwide — not a diagnosis, but a useful starting point.

BMICategory
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 – 24.9Normal weight
25 – 29.9Overweight
30 and aboveObese

Is BMI accurate?

BMI doesn't distinguish muscle from fat, so very athletic people can score "overweight" despite low body fat. It also applies to adults only. Use it as a general health indicator, alongside other measures.
