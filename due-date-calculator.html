How Due Dates Are Calculated

Naegele's Rule (LMP): Add 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last menstrual period, adjusted for cycle length.

Conception Date: Add 266 days (38 weeks) from the estimated date of conception.

IVF Transfer: Count 266 days from conception, subtracting the embryo age (3 or 5 days) since conception occurred before transfer.

Trimesters:

• 1st Trimester: Weeks 1 – 12

• 2nd Trimester: Weeks 13 – 27

• 3rd Trimester: Weeks 28 – 40+

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is an estimated due date?
Only about 4–5% of babies are born on their exact due date. Most arrive within two weeks before or after. An ultrasound in the first trimester provides the most accurate estimate.
What if my cycle isn't 28 days?
This calculator adjusts the standard 280-day estimate based on your actual cycle length. Longer cycles push the due date later, shorter cycles pull it earlier.
What is full term?
Full term is defined as 39 weeks 0 days through 40 weeks 6 days. Early term is 37–38 weeks, and late term is 41 weeks.
