Statistics

Types of Averages

Mean (Average): Sum of all numbers ÷ Count of numbers

Median: Middle value when numbers are sorted

Mode: Most frequently occurring number

Range: Difference between highest and lowest values

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between mean and median?
Mean is the average (sum ÷ count). Median is the middle value when sorted. Median is better when there are outliers.
What's the mode?
Mode is the number that appears most frequently in your dataset.
