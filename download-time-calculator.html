Bits vs. Bytes: The Important Difference

Megabits (Mbps): Internet Service Providers advertise speeds in megabits (lowercase "b").

Megabytes (MB): File sizes are measured in bytes (capital "B"). There are 8 bits in 1 byte.

Example: A 100 Mbps internet connection downloads at a maximum theoretical speed of 12.5 Megabytes per second (100 ÷ 8).
