How It Works

Final Exam Score Needed: Uses the formula: Needed = (Desired Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Final Weight)) ÷ Final Weight

Weighted Grade: Each assignment's score is multiplied by its weight percentage, then all are summed to produce the overall weighted average.

Example: If you have an 82% and the final is worth 25%, you'd need a 96% on the final to finish with a 85.5% overall.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I need more than 100% on my final?
This means your desired grade isn't achievable with a perfect final exam score. Consider adjusting your target grade or checking for extra credit opportunities.
Do assignment weights need to add up to 100%?
Ideally yes. If they don't, the calculator will still compute a weighted average based on the proportions you enter.
