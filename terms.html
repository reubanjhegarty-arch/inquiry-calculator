Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2023

At Inquiry Calculator, accessible from inquirycalculator.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Inquiry Calculator and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information essential to running the application. If you create an account, we store your authentication token via Google Firebase. Chat history may be stored locally on your device or linked to your account to provide continuity across sessions.

  • Local Storage: Used to track theme preferences and guest AI usage limits (3 questions/day).
  • Account Data: Email and UID if you choose to sign in via Firebase Auth.

2. Use of AI

Our AI solver utilizes third-party infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers AI). Prompts submitted to the AI may be processed on their servers to generate your response. Please do not submit sensitive personal information (PII) to the AI chat.

3. Cookies

Like any other website, Inquiry Calculator uses 'cookies' and browser storage to optimize user experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.
