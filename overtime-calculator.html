Standard Overtime Multipliers

Time-and-a-Half (1.5x): Base Rate × 1.5. Typically applies to hours worked past standard daily thresholds (e.g. beyond 8 or 38 hours) or on Saturdays.

Double Time (2.0x): Base Rate × 2.0. Typically applies to work on Sundays, public holidays, or excessive overtime periods.
