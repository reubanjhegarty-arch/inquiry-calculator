How It Works: The Mifflin-St Jeor Formula

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate): The minimum energy your body burns simply staying alive at complete rest.

TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure): Your BMR multiplied by your physical activity factor. Eating at your TDEE keeps your weight stable; eating below causes weight loss.
