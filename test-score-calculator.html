Frequently Asked Questions

How is the percentage calculated?
Percentage = (Questions Correct ÷ Total Questions) × 100. This gives you the raw score percentage before any curve.
Does this account for partial credit?
Enter your total earned points as "Questions Correct" and total possible points as "Total Questions" to handle partial credit situations.
