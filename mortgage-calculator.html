How Mortgages Are Calculated

Principal & Interest: M = P × [r(1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n - 1], where P is loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Escrow Costs: Property taxes and insurance are split into 12 equal monthly instalments and added to the principal and interest payment.
