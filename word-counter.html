Frequently Asked Questions

How many words is a typical essay?
A standard essay is 250–500 words (1–2 pages). A college essay is typically 500–1,500 words. A research paper ranges from 3,000–10,000 words.
How many pages is 1,000 words?
Approximately 2 pages single-spaced or 4 pages double-spaced in a standard 12pt font with normal margins.
