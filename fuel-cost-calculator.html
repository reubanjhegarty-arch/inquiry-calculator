How to Calculate Fuel Consumption

Litres per 100km (L/100km): Total Litres = (Distance in km ÷ 100) × Fuel Consumption Rate.

Miles per Gallon (MPG): Total Gallons = Distance in miles ÷ MPG rating.

Trip Cost = Total Fuel Required × Fuel Price per unit.
