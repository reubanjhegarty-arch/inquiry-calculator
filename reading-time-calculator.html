Frequently Asked Questions

How many words per page in a typical book?
A standard paperback has about 250 words per page. Academic texts may have 300–400 words per page due to smaller fonts and denser formatting.
How long does it take to read a 300-page book?
At an average reading speed of 225 wpm with 250 words per page, a 300-page book would take about 5 hours and 33 minutes to read.
